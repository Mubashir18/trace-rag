## SUMMARY
An actor tracked here as SLATE PLOVER (fictional) runs business e-mail compromise operations
against logistics companies using a kit called Pewterfly. Victims receive a shared-document notice
for an overdue invoice. The document page asks them to sign in and to open a "viewer" that steals
the mail client's access tokens. With those tokens the actor reads mailboxes, waits for a real
invoice thread, and then asks for payment to a new account. Stolen credentials have also been used
for remote log-ins from the actor's infrastructure.

## TECHNICAL DETAILS
### Initial Access
The notice links to secure-docshare[.]example[.]net, which imitates a document-sharing page
[T1566.002]. It offers `invoice_viewer.exe` "to open protected invoices" [T1204.002].
### Credential Access
The viewer reads the mail client's token cache [T1528] and posts the tokens to
pf-token-sink[.]example[.]com.
### Collection and Remote Access
Tokens are replayed from 203.0.113.142 to read mail [T1114.002]. The same address is later used to
log in remotely with stolen passwords [T1078].
