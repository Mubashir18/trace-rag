# Malicious Browser Extension "Fernwick" Exfiltrating Session Cookies

## ACTIONS TO TAKE TODAY
1. Search proxy logs for the network indicators in Table 1.
2. Search EDR alerts for the installer hash in Table 2 and remove the extension from matching hosts.
3. Sign all users of affected hosts out of web applications.

## SUMMARY
An actor tracked here as SEPIA AVOCET (fictional) distributes a browser extension called Fernwick
that claims to be a coupon helper. Once installed, it copies session cookies for business web
applications and sends them to the actor, who can then use the sessions without a password.
Travel agencies have been targeted most. Hosts that downloaded the extension or uploaded cookies
should be cleaned and their sessions revoked.

## TECHNICAL DETAILS
### Initial Access
Victims run `fernwick_installer.exe`, offered as a coupon tool on deal-sharing pages
[T1204.002]. The installer force-installs the extension from fw-ext-update[.]example[.]net
[T1176].
### Collection
The extension reads cookies for business web applications [T1539] and groups them by site.
### Exfiltration
Cookies are uploaded to cookie-vault-sync[.]example[.]com every hour [T1041]. When the domain is
blocked, the extension posts directly to 203.0.113.172.
Note: Fernwick mainly targets session cookies for the legitimate CRM crm[.]example[.]net, and it checks the legitimate status page status[.]example[.]org to decide when to upload. Visits to these services by themselves are normal.
