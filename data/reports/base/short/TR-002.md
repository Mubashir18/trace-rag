## SUMMARY
In early September 2026 an actor tracked here as SABLE HERON (fictional) compromised the build
pipeline of a small vendor of accounting plug-ins and published a tampered "Ledgerwick" plug-in
update. Finance teams in the retail sector that installed the update between 3 and 12 September
received a backdoor called Quillmoth alongside the genuine plug-in. Quillmoth gives the actor remote
command execution on the host. Any host that downloaded the tampered update or contacted the relay
below should be treated as compromised.

## TECHNICAL DETAILS
### Initial Access
The tampered update was served from a look-alike download host, ledgerpatch-dist[.]example[.]net,
which the plug-in's auto-updater was redirected to through a modified configuration file
[T1195.002]. The downloaded installer is named `lfplugin_setup.exe`.
### Execution and Persistence
The installer drops the legitimate plug-in and, in the same step, side-loads the Quillmoth DLL
through rundll32 [T1218.011]. Persistence is a Run registry key named after the vendor [T1547.001].
### Command and Control
Quillmoth polls qm-relay[.]example[.]org over HTTPS every five minutes [T1071.001]. When the relay
name cannot be resolved it connects directly to 203.0.113.112.
