## SUMMARY
Biotech companies have reported research data being copied to personal cloud storage shortly
before staff left for a competitor. In the cases summarised here, a portable sync tool called
Wickerlane (a fictional product) was used to upload shared-drive folders to a personal drive
service controlled by an outside party, here tracked as PALE HARRIER (fictional). The outside party
also logged in remotely with the insiders' accounts. Hosts that ran the tool or reached the storage
service need review.

bucket wl-sync-bucket[.]example[.]net [T1567.002]. Some hosts only resolved the bucket name
before the upload was blocked.
